The Art of Video Editing

Video editing is the process of manipulating and rearranging video shots to create a new cohesive work. It involves selecting the best clips, cutting footage, adding transitions, color grading, and mixing audio to shape the final narrative.

Stages of Post-Production

The post-production editing workflow typically follows a structured sequence:

  • Logging and Ingesting: Organizing raw footage and selecting the best takes.
  • Rough Cut: Assembling the narrative sequence chronologically based on the script.
  • Fine Cut: Trimming frame-by-frame for precise pacing, rhythm, and timing.
  • Color Correction and Audio Mixing: Balancing lighting tones and mastering sound clarity.
