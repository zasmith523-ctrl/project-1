The Sport of Volleyball

Volleyball is a fast-paced team sport in which two teams of six players are separated by a net. Each team tries to ground a ball on the other team's court under organized rules. It requires high athleticism, precise coordination, teamwork, and quick reflexes.

Core Fundamentals

Mastering the game relies heavily on several foundational techniques:

  • Serving: Initiating the rally from behind the baseline.
  • Passing (Bump): Receiving and controlling the opponent's serve or attack.
  • Setting: Positioning the ball precisely for an attacker to score.
  • Spiking (Attack): Hitting the ball forcefully over the net into the opponent's court.
